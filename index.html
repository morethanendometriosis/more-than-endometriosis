Why we’re here

Endometriosis is more than a medical condition.

It can shape how someone feels, plans, works, connects with others and imagines their future. Too often, those wider experiences are overlooked.

More Than Endometriosis is a growing initiative with a vision to bring people together, improve understanding and campaign for meaningful change — while creating space for honest conversations and compassionate support.

From awareness to action

Awareness is important.
Change is essential.

We want to help turn lived experience into action: better support, better understanding and a stronger voice for everyone affected by endometriosis.

What we want to champion

  • Better access to appropriate mental health support
  • More informed, compassionate fertility conversations
  • Greater understanding and support at work
  • Listening to people with lived experience
  • More research, recognition and accountability
